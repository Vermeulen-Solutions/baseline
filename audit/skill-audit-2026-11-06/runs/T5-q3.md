## Answer

Context: four routes, one site, no canvas requested. AGENTS.md routes this to new-site-workflow (Express track, since it has at most six routes) and then site-art-direction. The Art Direction Record must be saved in AGENTS.md and `npm run check:art-direction -- --require-record` run before any visual work. Proposals only below, nothing implemented.

Design problem: visitors mainly want (1) opening hours for two shops and (2) what is baked each day. Trust comes from real bread, ovens, staff and a hand-drawn logo. The supplied material is strong, so the design should show it and not decorate around it.

**Design surface (concept checkpoint, offered once):** HTML/browser concepts, the default when no canvas is requested. Brilliant, OpenPencil and Penpot are named once and OpenDesign stays optional. I would still compare two or three structurally distinct directions side by side before choosing one. Directions must differ in idea and structure, not only colour.

**1. Aesthetic lane: Custom / existing brand, with a light Editorial Paper inflection (about 70/30).**
- Reason: the lane reference says to use Custom for organic hospitality or photography-led retail when the four profiles force the wrong identity. Technical Systems, Product-Led Minimalism and Luminous Futurism clearly do not fit a family bakery.
- Editorial Paper is the nearest profile (warm paper canvas, hairline rules, spacious reading), but a pure Editorial Paper would feel like a publication or bookshop. So: warm flour/bone canvas, deep crust-brown ink, and one baked accent (terracotta or crust amber, not vermilion). Sharp or very small radii, hairline rules.
- The hand-drawn logo is a fixed brand commitment. It is preserved unchanged, not redrawn (brand-assets reference). I would extract a simplified favicon variant only if the full mark fails at 16/24/32px, and label it as a proposed variant.
- Narrative metaphor: the early-morning bake sheet and chalkboard "baked today" list, with oven-warm light. In practice it shapes the daily-bakes list and the hours table, which look like a hand-kept bake sheet. It stays absent from functional elements such as navigation, forms and the hours data semantics.

**2. Hero visual strategy: Art-Directed Photography / Editorial Frame.**
- Reason: the skill assigns this strategy to hospitality, physical-goods and craft brands. The live-coded UI specimen and the capability stage are for SaaS and agencies, so they are rejected here.
- Use one of the supplied bread or oven photos, prepared with `npm run media:prepare`, with `loading="eager" fetchpriority="high"` for LCP. Frame it with a deliberate aspect ratio, a hairline border and a small archival caption such as "FIG. 01 - the morning bake". Use a real caption, not invented facts.
- The hero must also carry the visitor's two tasks. Next to or under the photo, put a live-text "Open today" strip for both shops with real hours, plus a link to today's bakes. Hours are text, never baked into an image. Use `.tabular-nums` for times.
- Each image slot gets a distinct photo, with no photo repeated on the page. Staff and oven photos go to the story page and a secondary band.
- No stock art, since real photography exists. Staff are named only if the bakery supplies the names.

**3. Header archetype: Editorial Hairline Masthead.**
- Reason: it matches the Editorial Paper inflection. The hand-drawn logo sits at left on a flat paper ground, with hairline rules and four nav items (Home, Breads and pastries, Shops and hours, Story).
- Adaptation for the main task: a compact "Open now / today's hours" element in the masthead or just under it, linking to Shops and hours. Numbered nav indices are optional and I would drop them, since they are a telemetry-style device and the voice is warm.
- Rejected: Floating Glass Capsule (glass reads as tech and fights the paper and photo ground) and Exposed Brutalist bar (wrong tone).
- Real navigation semantics and a visible focus state are kept. A sticky header (`data-sticky-header`) is acceptable only if it stays light, so the hours remain reachable on mobile.

**4. Expressive levels (reasons are site-specific)**
- Composition: moderate (orderly, with deliberate variety). Orderly because visitors are scanning for hours and products. Varied enough that the pages do not stack identical card rows. Planned pacing: Home = asymmetric photo hero, then a hairline "today's bakes" ledger, then a dark-surface spotlight band (oven photo or story teaser), then a shops/hours closing band. Breads page = a filterable or grouped list (bread, pastry) with photos in varied spans, not three equal columns repeated. Shops page = two shop panels side by side, each with an hours table, address and map link. Story = narrative flow with a pull-photo and a drop-cap-style essay opening.
- Motion: quiet. Only feedback, gentle `data-reveal` entrances on photos, and hover or focus states on links and buttons. Reduced-motion is honoured and the page must be complete without motion. No parallax or scroll-jacking, and no autoplay. Motion does not serve the visitor's tasks here, and a motion request has not been made.
- Density: balanced overall, but information-rich where it counts. Hours and the daily bakes list are compact, scannable and tabular. The story page is spacious and reading-led (about 60-68ch measure). This matches the Express default of balanced when the brief is silent, with the exceptions explained by the visitor tasks. Spatial Density in the record: Standard.

**5. Display typography settings**
- Display (`--font-display`): a warm, high-contrast or soft serif with real character (candidates to test, subject to licence, French/diacritic coverage and available weights, served via Astro's `fonts` API with `<Font preload />`: Fraunces, Newsreader, or a similar soft serif). Fixed tokens: tracking -0.03em to -0.04em, line-height 0.95-1.08, `text-wrap: balance`. Do not tighten so far that accented capitals or descenders collide.
- Inline accent voice (`.font-accent`, italic serif): used on one or two focal words in the home h1 and maybe one h2, for human warmth. Keep inline spans on one line (Astro v7 whitespace rule).
- Body (`--font-body`): a high-x-height, neutral humanist sans, line-height 1.6-1.68, measure 60-68ch, so hours and product descriptions stay legible on a phone.
- Mono/telemetry voice: use sparingly. `.tabular-nums` is mandatory for hours and prices. A small uppercase tracked eyebrow (0.75rem, 0.12em) may label "TODAY" or shop names but with warm wording, no `[01 // ...]` index codes.
- Rationale: it pairs the bakery's human, hand-made character (matching the hand-drawn logo) with a clear reading and data voice. The type must complement the hand-drawn logo without imitating it, and I would not use a script face as display.
- Typography is open here, so I would show a short specimen of 3-4 pairings with real headline, intro, buttons and an hours row, so the owner picks type separately from layout.

**6. Site anti-patterns (3, each with a reason)**
- No stock bakery imagery, wheat-stalk or chalkboard clip-art, or faux-rustic textures. Real photos of the bread, ovens and staff exist and carry authenticity. Repeating a generic bakery cliche would make the site belong to any bakery.
- No hours or daily-bake information inside images, carousels or hidden behind accordions or popups. This is the visitors' main task, so it needs to be live HTML text, visible on first view and on mobile, in one consistent place.
- No glassmorphism, neon or tech-style gradients, and no repeated identical three-column card grids. They contradict the warm craft lane and the skill's pacing rule (no consecutive identical 3-column geometry). I would also not invent testimonials, awards, founding dates or staff names that the short history does not supply.

**Proposed Art Direction Record (to save in AGENTS.md before visual work)**
- Aesthetic Lane: Custom / existing brand (organic hospitality) with a light Editorial Paper inflection. The bakery's hand-drawn logo and photography are the visual anchor.
- Narrative Metaphor: the morning bake sheet and chalkboard list under oven-warm light. It shapes the daily-bakes list, hours table and photo captions, and stays out of navigation and forms.
- Typography Pairing: Display: warm soft/high-contrast serif (e.g. Fraunces or Newsreader, to confirm), Body: neutral humanist sans. Italic accent on 1-2 words, tabular numerals for times.
- Token Physics: Canvas: warm flour/bone paper, dark crust-brown ink. Action/Accent: one baked terracotta or amber, checked for AA contrast. Borders: hairline 1px with sharp or tiny radius. Spatial Density: Standard (compact on hours/bakes, spacious on story).
- Site Anti-Patterns: stock or clip-art bakery cliches and faux-rustic textures; hours or daily bakes hidden in images, sliders or accordions; glass, neon and identical repeated 3-column card rows.

**Assumptions to confirm (reversible):** French-first or single locale as the bakery decides; opening hours and the daily-bake list come from real owner data (no invented hours or products); the "baked each day" list is edited as file-owned content or CMS data depending on the platform profile chosen. Static or Sveltia is likely enough for four pages unless the bakery needs a live editing canvas, which was not mentioned.
