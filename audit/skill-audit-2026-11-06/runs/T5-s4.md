## Answer

Routing: a four-route, one-locale greenfield site is Express-sized (new-site-workflow). Art direction comes before visual implementation, and the Art Direction Record must be saved in AGENTS.md and gated with `npm run check:art-direction -- --require-record`. Nothing is implemented here. No canvas was requested, so the design surface defaults to HTML/browser concepts (offered once, with Brilliant, OpenPencil and Penpot named). Two or three structurally distinct directions are compared side by side before one is chosen.

Design problem: visitors want opening hours for two shops and what is baked each day. That is a scan-and-find task. The brand is warm and craft-led, and the real photography of bread, ovens and staff, the hand-drawn logo and the family history carry the identity.

**1. Aesthetic lane: Custom / existing brand (organic hospitality), with a light Editorial Paper inflection (about 70/30).**
- Reason: aesthetic-lanes.md says to use Custom when the four profiles force the wrong identity, and names organic hospitality and photography-led retail as examples. Technical Systems, Product-Led Minimalism and Luminous Futurism are wrong for a bakery.
- Editorial Paper is the closest profile: warm unbleached canvas, ink text, hairline rules. Its sharp corners and strict serif voice are too cold, so the inflection is limited to typography and rules.
- The hand-drawn logo is a supplied master. It is preserved unchanged and sets the warmth, with no replacement identity. A small-size variant is a proposal only.
- Narrative metaphor: the baker's morning bake board and flour-dusted paper bag labels. It shows in the daily "today's bake" list and in ledger-style hours. It stays out of navigation semantics and out of the logo.
- Palette roles: warm flour or bone canvas, dark crust-brown ink, one baked-crust or terracotta action accent.

**2. Hero visual strategy: (d) Art-directed photography / editorial frame, using real prepared photography.**
- Reason: the offer is a place, physical product and craft, and usable photography exists. Under the take-the-first-match rule, (a) no fixed treatment applies, (b) and (c) do not fit, and (d) matches.
- Use one real oven or bread photo, prepared with `npm run media:prepare`, with a deliberate aspect ratio, a hairline border and a caption. No stock art.
- The hero also carries the visitor's task: today's opening-hours status and a "today's bake" link sit beside or under the photo, so the first proof is real and useful. The staff photos go on the story page, and no photo repeats on one page.

**3. Header archetype: Editorial Hairline Masthead.**
- Reason: the default pairing for Editorial Paper is the hairline masthead, and glass is an Editorial Paper anti-pattern. With only four top-level items, the capsule's width limits do not apply.
- Use the hand-drawn logo as the wordmark. Navigation numbering is optional and should stay plain, since decorative indices are not wanted here.
- Keep a persistent "Hours" link or today's open/closed status in the header, because hours are the main visitor need.

**4. Expressive levels**
- Composition: low-to-moderate (orderly). Visitors complete a task (hours) and compare items (the daily bake), and the skill says variation costs scanning speed. Spend variation on the story and home photo moments, and keep shops/hours and breads orderly. Still vary section geometry on home (split photo hero, hairline ledger, photo spotlight) and avoid consecutive identical 3-card rows.
- Motion: quiet. The audience is task-focused and broad, including older visitors. Use feedback only (hover, focus, a gentle image reveal at most), with reduced-motion honoured at every level. No motion is requested, so there is no capability-routing detour.
- Density: moderate and information-forward on the shops-and-hours page and on the breads page, where the content is a scanned schedule and a comparable catalogue (tabular, `.tabular-nums` for times, hairline rows). Spacious, reading-led on the story and home. Express records brief-led density as balanced, so the Record's Spatial Density is Standard.

**5. Display typography settings**
- Display voice: a warm, characterful serif such as Fraunces or Newsreader (from the Editorial Paper list). The font must be one the project can legally serve, with reliable weights and French accent coverage verified. The site is French-first, so check that glyphs such as é, è, ç, œ render well. Body is a high-x-height neutral sans such as Inter, at 1.6-1.68 line-height and a 60-68ch measure.
- Display settings: tracking -0.03em, or a lighter -0.02em if the serif is already tightly fitted. Line-height 0.95-1.08, nudged toward 1.05-1.08 for accents and descenders in French. `text-wrap: balance`.
- Italic accent voice: `<em class="font-accent">` on one or two focal words in an h1 or h2, which suits the warm editorial feel.
- Times and prices use `.tabular-nums` so the hours table aligns. The telemetry-mono eyebrow is used only with real, meaningful labels, for example "Shop 1 / Shop 2", and is dropped if it feels technical.

**6. Site anti-patterns (three, specific to this site)**
- No stock-bakery imagery (generic wheat, croissant or flat-lay stock) when real photos of their bread, ovens and staff exist.
- No glassmorphism or floating capsule header, no neon or tech-startup gradients, and no cold dark-mode tech surfaces. They contradict the warm craft lane and the Editorial Paper header rule.
- No hiding hours behind a map-pin widget, carousel, hover or accordion, and no identical three-card section runs. Opening hours stay plain text, visible on first screen and in tabular form. Also no invented testimonials, awards or "since" claims beyond the supplied history.

**Record to save (draft)**
- Aesthetic Lane: Custom / existing brand (organic craft hospitality) with Editorial Paper inflection.
- Narrative Metaphor: the baker's morning bake board and paper bag labels.
- Typography Pairing: warm serif display with neutral sans body.
- Token Physics: Canvas warm flour; Accent crust or terracotta; Borders hairline 1px, small radius; Spatial Density Standard.
- Site Anti-Patterns: the three bans above.

Assumptions to label and confirm: font choice and its licence and French coverage; photo source widths at hero size; the hand-drawn logo supplied as vector or only raster (raster would mean preserving it as is and proposing a clean vector variant separately).
