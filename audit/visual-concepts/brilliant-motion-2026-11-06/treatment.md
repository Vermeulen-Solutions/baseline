# Baseline workflow motion study

Reading this as: a UI feature tour for a site owner, at 120 BPM, in the existing Baseline dark/lime palette, leaning on native layout and type, after the Baseline site mockup. This is a mechanical prototype, not a new campaign or approved site copy.

6 seconds, 960×540, 30 fps, silent. The focal motion follows the active step from brief to concepts to Astro proof. Shared highlight geometry explains continuity; text and colour identify the current state. Native motion is a film, not browser button behavior.

States: 01 Brief held 0–1.5s; move to 02 Concepts 1.5–2.0s; hold to 3.5s; move to 03 Astro proof 3.5–4.0s; final held to 6s. Each move is 0.5s with deceleration, 3-sample motion blur. No camera moves, bounce, audio, gratuitous looping or width-driven text reflow. The stable step labels remain readable.

Inputs: existing Baseline mark-as-letter, dark page #0b0e0d, panel #171b18, ink #e5e6e2, muted #adb5ab, action #c5ff00. Arial is the same mechanical-study face used by the preceding native capability test; production typography remains owned by the site.

Reduced-motion review: use a held final state as the poster. The companion browser prototype must select states immediately under prefers-reduced-motion, while preserving controls and all content.

Checks: no elastic spring; no 3D flip; no reversing camera; no animated text wrapping; two transitions of the same style; final state held for 2s. Slower reading holds are deliberate for this UI explanation; the vendor launch-film energy heuristics are diagnostics rather than authority to add decoration.
