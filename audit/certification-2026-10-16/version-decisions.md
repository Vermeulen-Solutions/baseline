Owner: starter maintainer, certification implemented with Codex. Availability observations are in `upstream.json`; they are not compatibility claims. Official Astro Docs and EmDash Docs MCP queries succeeded during this review. Installed packages and lockfile supply the tested versions; a newer release triggers evaluation rather than an automatic upgrade.
