## Start with the outcome

Describe the change the project should make for the people using it.

Keep the brief close to the work so the team can revisit its **purpose** as decisions change.

### Three useful questions

1. Who needs the result?
2. What should become easier?
3. How will the team recognize a useful outcome?

> A short brief can still make the important choices explicit.
