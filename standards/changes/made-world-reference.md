# One made-world site sets the level, case study or not

Two design trials (a restaurant and a plumber) spent about half of each run on reference research, most of it proving that a strong site was a studio's case study: studio pages rarely link the client's live site, and one trial ended up selecting the same site twice, its live pages and its studio's case study, to satisfy the rule.

- `check:art-direction --require-references` asks for at least one selected real site that scores 3 on craft, a made world, of any kind; a studio case study is no longer required. The guidance keeps case studies as the usual place to find one, adds the route that worked (from a strong site of the trade to the studio that built it), and counts two captures of the same site as one lesson.
- A source cell may carry a note after its URL in the gate as well as in `audit:composition`.
- The concept-bar message says the bar may be reached in the built page scored with the Stage 5 comparison, and a passing run with `--require-comparison` points at Stage 5's critique and Stage 6 instead of Stage 4.5.
