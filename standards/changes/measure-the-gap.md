# The page is measured against its references, and references are found where the level is

A trial built with professional photographs reached a made world (a felling tape across the page, numbered log tags, a trunk measuring tool, five named motions running) and still sat well below the studio-built sites it aimed at: 2 drawn objects against their 10, 19 running animations against 136, 191 elements against 654. The sheets show the difference; nothing put a number on it. And its reference research scored 62 candidates over eight rounds before three passed, because the card rejected strong sites outright for a label over each heading or a consent panel.

- `audit:composition -- --references audit/references/references.md` measures each selected reference's live page the same way as the page (at the wide width, consent declined) and prints the page beside the references' median on drawn objects, running animations, rotated or masked objects, surfaces, colours, type sizes and elements, with an arrow where the page has under half. Advisory; the Stage 5 comparison runs it and gives the measures to the reviewer, and the five additions answer the arrows with objects, layers and motions from the world.
- Reference research starts with studio case studies and gallery categories (their later pages too), then the trade. The template look is scored on the composition line instead of rejecting the site, and a consent panel the capture cannot answer is hidden with `--hide` and the page behind it judged.
- `capture:routes` no longer turns lazy images with `sizes="auto, …"` eager before a full-page capture: that made the browser pick another source and paint the photograph blank.
- The recorded World's display line may go past the craft floor's 6rem cap (up to eight times the body size, capped at `17vi`); the two texts said different things.
- The font recipe names the files-in-`src/styles/fonts/` way that `design:tokens` and the starter's token tests both read.
