# The copy guides and the pacing blueprint are on the path a new site follows

Two sites were built by a weaker model that was told only to follow the workflow as written. Its sessions were traced for which guides it opened. Both runs opened 18 of the 38 guides on the design path, and neither opened a single copy guide.

The cause is in the routing, not in the model. `copywriting.md` and `copy-voice.md` were named only in the workflow skill's table of conditional references ("read only the one that matches the request"), and no stage reference linked them. A builder that follows the stage map never reaches them, although every new site writes its pages' words.

- The stage map names the two copy guides at Stage 3 and `copy-voice.md` again at Stage 5.
- Stage 3 in `brief-platform-content.md` and Stage 5 in `build-verify-launch.md` link them where the words are drafted and set.
- Express Checkpoint A names them, and Checkpoint C begins with Stage 5 of `build-verify-launch.md`: its summary sent a builder straight to the routes, past the pacing blueprint.
- The conditional row now says it is for copy work on its own.

Not changed here: which references of the site-design skill a new site must open. Both runs skipped most of them too, and that is settled with the rest of the workflow documents.
